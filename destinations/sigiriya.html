About Sigiriya

Sigiriya is one of Sri Lanka's most famous historical landmarks. Located in the Matale District of the Central Province, it is renowned for its enormous rock formation, ancient urban planning, gardens, frescoes, and archaeological remains.

The site combines natural scenery with remarkable examples of ancient engineering and artistic achievement. Visitors can explore the surrounding gardens and climb the rock to discover the remains of the historic complex near its summit.

History of Sigiriya

Sigiriya is closely associated with King Kashyapa, who established his royal residence there in the fifth century CE.

The archaeological landscape includes landscaped gardens, water features, defensive structures, and the remains of buildings constructed around and on the rock.

Sigiriya is also recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and is an important destination for understanding Sri Lanka's ancient history and architectural heritage.

Main Attractions

The Lion Stairway

Discover the famous entrance area associated with the enormous lion structure that once formed part of the approach to the summit.

Ancient Frescoes

Learn about the celebrated paintings preserved on the rock and their significance in Sri Lankan art history.

Water Gardens

Explore the landscaped gardens, pools, and water features that demonstrate the site's impressive historic planning.

Visitor Information

Location

Near Sigiriya, Matale District, Sri Lanka.

Best Time to Visit

Early morning is often a good choice for cooler temperatures and a more comfortable climb. Weather conditions vary.

Recommended Preparation

Wear comfortable walking shoes, bring drinking water, and prepare for stairs and exposed sections of the climb.

Check current ticket prices, opening hours, access restrictions, and visitor guidance with official sources before your trip.

Travel Tips

  • Start early to avoid the hottest part of the day.
  • Wear comfortable shoes suitable for climbing stairs.
  • Bring water and sun protection.
  • Follow site rules and respect protected heritage.
  • Allow enough time to explore the surrounding gardens.
  • Check weather conditions before visiting.
